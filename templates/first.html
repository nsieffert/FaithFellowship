First Time Visiting? We love visitors!

Sunday School begins at 9:45 AM on Sundays, and there is coffee and donuts.

Our service begins at 10:45 AM on Sundays and ends at 11:45 AM. Yes, we keep it to one hour.

Everyone dresses comfortably. There is no dress code. Come as you are.

We do make it a point to welcome our guests. You will not be ignored!


Feel free to ask questions or get involved.

The Church of the Nazarene invites all who profess Jesus to participate in Holy Communion, even if they are not members.

Let us know if you need a nursery for your children.


We have Sunday School classes for older children. This begins at 9:45 AM. Then the older children will attend the main service.
