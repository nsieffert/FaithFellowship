June 16, 2026 - For the last several years I have enjoyed having my chickens free ranging in the back yard. They are so funny to watch! But then an owl showed up. We never saw him but we heard him in the early morning hours. And suddenly my hens began to disappear one at a time. By the time I realized what was happening I had lost several good laying birds.

So it was with sadness that I cleaned out the coop and caught each bird one at a time to lock them back up. They are safer now, but I miss having them run to me when I come out to feed the animals in the morning.
June 15, 2026 - There has been a lot in the news lately about UFO's, especially since the white house has been releasing its files and videos. What are they? Is it possible that there is other life that we don't know about? Of course it's possible. Nowhere does the Bible tell us that there is nothing else but us. So yes, it's possible. God can do anything He wants.

But is it reasonable? God made this world for us, and I don't believe He would allow alien life to invade. So what other explanation could there be? It could be spiritual in nature. I have two reason for saying this. First, in centuries past, the alien technology seemed to match the technology of the age. For example, aliens in the 18th century did not go whizzing across the sky at fast speeds. They were much slower. And second, there are hundreds of accounts of people who have stopped an alien attack by simply calling on Jesus.
June 14, 2026 - We all have days when we just don't feel like it. Feel like what? Feel like anything. God knows our frame, says Psalm 103:14, that we are but dust. Well, sometimes dust just doesn't feel like doing anything.

But this is why God gave us the Sabbath. I don't believe we have to keep it as strictly as the Israelites did, where we couldn't prepare a meal or even light a fire. But I do believe that we all need to have a day where we just kind of rest and relax, doing things we enjoy.
June 13, 2026 - I have a birdfeeder outside my window (not this one, but something similar) and I love watcing the birds come and perch while they eat. It is so peaceful to watch them, even when they fight with each other for the best position!

It reminds me of how we ought to be, without the fighting part, of course! But trusting little ones coming to our heavenly Father each morning for our own meal - actual food and spiritual nourishment. God will give us as much as we need because He is a loving heavenly Father!
June 12, 2026 - I first discovered Apologetics in 2010. I had been a Christian for several decades, but always had doubts. Sometimes I caught myself hedging my bets, just in case Christianity wasn't true, because no one had ever shown me any real evidence. Until 2010. That's when I heard R.C. Sproul talking about evidence for the Christian faith! I couldn't believe it. It was actually true!

I began to study apologetics and eventually got my doctorate in it. I have never looked back, and I have no doubts about Christianity at all. If you want to know more just reach out! I love talking about the evidence for Christianity!

I admit I had fun watching my chicks out in the rain. They are young and this is their first rain storm. They didn't know what to make of it. But I gather that they didn't like it very much! LOL. 🤣

It is so great to know that whatever else might happen, I can't make God stop loving me. What a joyous release it is to not have to worry about that any more forever! God is great!

The earth is truly filled with His glory, and we can only witness a smidgeon of it. But He puts it all out there every single day. He has created a beautiful planet for us. Enjoy!

March 15, 2026 - What does the name "The Church of the Nazarene mean? Jesus, our Lord and Savior is the Nazarene. We are the people of Jesus - we are the church of the "Nazarene" Himself. We sometimes call ourselves "Nazarenes" but that is not strictly correct. We are of the same beliefs as THE Nazarene - Jesus. But we are not THE Nazarene. We are His followers.

The Church of the Nazarene is a holiness church in the Wesleyan tradition. We beleive in the same things that were taught by John Wesley and the Methodists of the 18th century. We take our holiness seriously, and we wish for all people to do the same. But we are not judgmental. We are saved by grace alone like every Christian.
